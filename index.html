FlightSpot

Point your phone at the sky to see which planes are up there: airline, flight, type and distance, from live ADS-B data.

Uses the camera, compass and location. Your position, rounded to about 1 km, is sent to adsb.lol to ask for nearby planes. Flight data: adsb.lol (ODbL); routes: adsbdb.com.

Source

Live positions come from adsb.lol, a free community ADS-B network (data under the ODbL licence). The feed doesn't allow calls from a web page, so live traffic needs the Android app; the web version shows simulated traffic.
